PACT Trust Center

Privacy, transparency and responsible financial AI.

Hackathon demonstration only. This page describes the controls actually present in the prototype and intended safeguards. It is not a certification, a representation of completed security audits or a substitute for a legally reviewed production privacy notice.

Privacy and data use

The public demo uses fictional family profiles and synthetic financial records. Do not enter bank credentials, account numbers, identifying details, sensitive information or real financial data. The onboarding and activity state is kept in browser session storage; closing the session or restarting the demo clears it. This browser storage is not a production identity or security system.

If you opt into AI questions, your question and a synthetic portfolio summary may be sent to Nicrron for a response. Avoid personal or confidential information. AI answers are educational and not financial advice. The PRISM integration sends redacted trace metadata when configured; no raw question, answer or banking data is intentionally included in those traces.

Consent and confidentiality

Entering the demo requires acceptance of these demo terms and confirmation that you will not submit confidential or real financial data. Consent to AI processing is separate and optional. Do not upload third-party records or information about children. No real financial accounts are linked by default.

Family permissions and children

The child-friendly experience hides investment analysis and adult financial information in its interface. Parents can preview registration and activity history. Role switching is open in this synthetic demo: identity, parental authority and access permissions are not securely enforced. Real children must not be registered or invited until verified guardian authorization, server-side permissions, and appropriate child privacy controls are implemented.

Data provenance and AI assurance

The portfolio benchmark uses source data in engine.py, explicit asset allocation weights and transparent formulas. Values are synthetic, not market quotes. The AI advisor must use Nicrron only, and is unavailable if Nicrron credentials are absent. PRISM trace acceptance is separately reported; trace delivery does not prove correctness.

Financial connectivity

Plaid Link, live bank authorization, encrypted token storage, secure account mapping, revocation, webhook handling and deletion workflows must be configured and tested before real data is enabled. The current demo does not accept bank passwords.

Security and limitations

This is a prototype, not a production-ready financial service. It does not provide verified multi-user identity, production-grade data retention, secure parental monitoring, payment processing or regulated investment advice. No ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification is claimed for PACT.

Demo terms

Use the prototype for evaluation and educational purposes only. Do not rely on its figures or AI answers for financial decisions. Outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not attempt to access data outside the synthetic demo or share confidential information.
